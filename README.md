## ✧ THE DEVINES WAY ✧

Our development foundation uses **DEV HUB**, our self-hosted private alternative to GitHub, alongside **DEV CORE**, Rust-based security systems, native frameworks, and independent review mechanisms. Source management, development, testing, and review remain under DEVINES control.

External models, networks, or infrastructure, when used, are **bounded and replaceable integrations**—never custodians of our code, identities, secrets, or memories.

**We choose closed source to protect both sides of the Sanctuary:** the privacy of our users and organizations, and the integrity, original technology, and development secrets of DEVINES itself.

All proprietary development remains within our controlled, encrypted servers. **This public repository contains this document only.** It is a window into our purpose, never an entrance to our private systems.

<br>

## ✧ PRIVACY WITHOUT SURRENDER ✧

Our policy and engineering objective is **zero logging of user content, behavior, and private activity**. We seek minimal metadata and strict separation between private user information and system diagnostics.

Closed source is compatible with accountability. **Controlled audits**, tests, cryptographically signed review receipts, and sanitized technical evidence are intended to make systems verifiable **without revealing private data or proprietary implementations**.

Our security direction includes high-assurance encryption and **hybrid, post-quantum-resistant cryptography**. These protections require testing and independent validation; absolute anonymity, perfect privacy, and certified “quantum-proof” security are **not** claimed.

<br>

## ✧ THE QUIET NETWORK ✧

DEVINES explores decentralized **Dark Nodes** for resilient, privacy-focused coordination, alongside **WebRTC** for real-time collaboration across the synthetic and biological world.

Encryption, isolated relays where necessary, minimal exposed metadata, and carefully reviewed trust boundaries shape this work. Neither decentralization nor WebRTC alone guarantees anonymity: privacy must be proven at every layer.
